Project Overview & Problem Statement

Understanding the healthcare needs during Pandharpur Wari and SAHYATRI's simple solution.

Key Challenges in Wari

Lakhs of pilgrims travel on foot during the annual Pandharpur Wari. Healthcare management faces major challenges:

  • High cases of dehydration, heat exhaustion, and foot injuries.
  • Paper-based medical logs lead to delayed communication between posts.
  • Elderly pilgrims usually do not have smartphones to request help.
  • Difficulty in tracking emergency locations along moving Dindi routes.

The SAHYATRI Solution

SAHYATRI connects pilgrims, field volunteers, and medical posts into one reliable system:

  1. SOS Smart Band (₹100–120): Simple button band with printed QR medical info.
  2. Volunteer App: Quick symptom logging & offline record storage.
  3. Central Map & Dashboard: Live route tracking & camp capacity view.
  4. Marathi IVR Phone Helpline: Toll-free number (1800-SAHYATRI) for feature phones.

System Components & Estimated Cost

Hardware Band

  • ESP32 / BLE module
  • CR2032 Coin battery
  • Emergency push button
  • Silicone band + QR Code

Volunteer Mobile App

  • Offline-first storage
  • BLE signal scanner
  • Simple Marathi form UI
  • Auto-sync on connectivity

Central Server

  • Node.js / Express backend
  • Route map & emergency alert feed
  • Medical stock status

Project Feasibility

  • Band Cost: ₹100 - ₹120
  • Works without pilgrim smartphone
  • Easy deployment across medical camps

Band Design Details

🏷️

Printed QR Medical ID

Volunteers can scan the band's printed QR code to immediately view the pilgrim's name, emergency contact, and basic medical details.

  • Battery Life: Runs for over 20 days on a single CR2032 coin cell.
  • Signal: Transmits Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) signal to nearby volunteers up to 50 meters.
  • Cost: Designed to cost between ₹100 and ₹120 per unit.
