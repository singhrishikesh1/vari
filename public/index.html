Stage 1 Problem Discovery & Solution Pitch

Comprehensive overview of challenges faced during Pandharpur Wari and SAHYATRI's low-cost solution.

⚠️ The Problem Statement

Every year, lakhs of devotees walk in the Pandharpur Wari. Medical camps currently depend on paper records, manual reporting, and phone calls.

  • Heatstroke, dehydration, diabetic complications & cardiac emergencies.
  • Volunteers lack real-time centralized tracking.
  • Elderly pilgrims often lack smartphones, making digital apps inaccessible.
  • Lack of early warning for disease outbreaks along the route.

💡 Proposed Ecosystem Solution

SAHYATRI introduces a 4-pillar offline-capable healthcare ecosystem designed specifically for the Wari environment:

  1. SOS Smart Band (₹100–120): Wearable BLE panic button with QR medical ID.
  2. Offline Volunteer App: Symptom logging & automatic sync.
  3. Central Health Dashboard: Live route monitoring & emergency alert map.
  4. IVR Accessibility Layer: Toll-free Marathi phone helpline (1800-SAHYATRI).

🛠️ Technology Stack & Feasibility

Hardware Architecture

  • ESP32 Microcontroller
  • BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy)
  • CR2032 Battery (Long Life)
  • Silicone Water-Resistant Band
  • Production: Nordic nRF52832

Volunteer Mobile App

  • Flutter Prototype
  • SQLite / Room DB
  • BLE Auto-Discovery
  • Offline Sync Queue
  • Marathi Language Support

Backend & Analytics

  • Node.js / Express Server
  • Firestore & WebSockets
  • Rule-based Cluster Detection
  • GeoJSON Route Mapping

Cost & Impact

  • Smart Band Cost: ₹100–120
  • Zero Pilgrim Smartphone Requirement
  • Scalable to Kumbh Mela & Amarnath Yatra
